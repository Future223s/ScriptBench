Sir I have sent youe a trewe Inventorie of my Mothers good{es}
I would have sent it sooner but I wanted a Convenient
messenger, youe may see by it that my Mothers substance was
not great, But that it was Cheefely in Heaven where I
Doubte not but shee hath the fruishion of them, I am Assured
shee ought not any thinge to any man but good will, & soe
with many thankes for my kinde entertaynment, I with
prayers to god for my Cozene youre wyves safe Delyverance
I Committ youe to the Almightie

	She that thinkes her
	selfe most bond to
	you for your fatherley
	kare: Iane Ashely
