With my very harty comendacons I haue herinclosed
sent you ye names of diuerse very dangerous recusant{es}
dwelling neare vnto wisbitch castell whome I praie you
to take some course for either by your self or some other
Iustic{es} of ye peace thereabowt{es} That they maie be bownde
over to appeare at ye next assizes to be holden for the
Cowntie of Norff to answere vnto such matters as shalbe
obiected against them on her maiesties behalf And even
so I bid you very hartely farewell; At Sergeant{es} Inn
this xxth of Iune 1599

    Your very loving frende
    Io: Popham
